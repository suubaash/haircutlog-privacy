Privacy Policy for Vocab: Words You Know

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Vocab: Words You Know is an offline iPhone app that estimates how many English words you know and lets you build a personal collection of words.

Data collected

Vocab does not collect any data. There is no account and no sign-up, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data stored on your device

Your word ratings, collection count, daily history, and streak are saved locally on your device only. The dictionary ships inside the app, so Vocab works entirely offline and your progress never leaves your device.

Third parties

No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party. Vocab does not sell or share any information, because none is collected.

Data deletion

Because everything is stored on your device, deleting the app permanently removes all Vocab data from your device.

Children

Vocab collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
