Vocab: Words You Know - Support

Vocab estimates how many English words you know and lets you keep collecting more. It runs entirely offline on your iPhone, with no account and no ads.

Frequently asked questions

How is my vocabulary size calculated? You rate a sample of about 40 words drawn from across the word-frequency spectrum, and the app extrapolates from your answers to estimate your total vocabulary. It is a statistical estimate, not an exact count.

Is my progress saved? Yes. Your collection count, daily history, and streak are stored on your device, so you can continue where you left off.

Do I need an internet connection? No. The dictionary is bundled with the app, so everything works offline.
